# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Content marketers and social media managers who have a blog post and need to repurpose it into platform-specific assets fast. They open the tool, paste a URL, and expect polished output in seconds. Speed and output quality are the only metrics they care about.

## Product Purpose

A single-purpose AI tool: give it a blog URL, get back LinkedIn posts, Twitter threads, SEO metadata, and a video script outline. Success means the generated content is ready to publish with minimal editing.

## Brand Personality

Polished, confident, direct. Professional without being corporate. Stripe/Notion energy: the UI gets out of the way and lets the output speak.

## Anti-references

- Generic SaaS cream: Inter everywhere, white background, soft purple gradients, rounded hero cards.
- AI startup glow: dark mode with neon particle effects, glowing orbs, animated gradients on text.
- Cute/bubbly consumer apps: pastel palettes, rounded-everything, emoji-heavy UI.

## Design Principles

1. **Output is the product.** The generated content section is the main event; the input form is just the gate.
2. **Speed reads as quality.** No unnecessary chrome, animations, or decorative layers. If it doesn't aid comprehension, remove it.
3. **Earn the user's trust with density.** Show the full outputs clearly and legibly; don't hide content behind collapsed states without reason.
4. **Consistency over novelty.** Reuse a tight set of typographic and spacing decisions rather than varying them for visual interest.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA. Standard keyboard navigation and focus states. No animation that cannot be disabled via `prefers-reduced-motion`.
